E-Commerce Profitability & Marketing Analysis

MySQL · Python/Pandas · Power BI

1. This dataset, obtained from Analyst Builder by Alex Freberg, is suitable for end-to-end e-commerce data analysis, from exploratory data analysis to business insights.
2. This analysis aims to uncover actionable insights from transaction, cost, return, and marketing data to evaluate profitability and support better business decision-making.
3. The e-commerce business generated approximately $236K in revenue and $56K in profit from 2,000 orders. Electronics contributed the highest profit, while Marketplace recorded the highest loss rate at 36.99%.
4. The analysis also identified low-performing category-channel combinations and low-ROAS marketing segments that could be optimized to improve profitability.
5. Using MySQL for data extraction and analysis, Python/Pandas for data exploration and diagnostic analysis, and Power BI for visualization and business insights.
Dataset Source: Analyst Builder — Alex Freberg

Loan Default Risk Analysis

MySQL · Python/Pandas · SciPy · Power BI

1. This project analyzes borrower profiles and loan application data to identify characteristics associated with loan default risk and support data-driven credit risk assessment.
2. The analysis focuses on credit score, debt-to-income (DTI) ratio, interest rate, employment characteristics, loan amount, and loan purpose to understand patterns in loan defaults.
3. The dataset contains 601 loan records, with 146 defaults and an overall default rate of 24.29%, exceeding the assumed target rate of 12%.
4. The analysis found that borrowers with credit scores of 520–599 had a 49.14% default rate, compared with 11.69% for borrowers with scores of 750+. DTI and interest rates also showed positive, statistically significant associations with default.
5. Using MySQL for data querying and validation, Python/Pandas for data exploration and analysis, SciPy for statistical testing, and Power BI for visualization and business insights.
5. The project translates analytical findings into practical recommendations, including enhanced review of high-DTI borrowers and multi-factor credit risk assessment.
Data Source: Analyst Builder — Alex Freberg
